# D-DASH-01 — Dashboard foundation (slice D0)

Owner instruction (2026-11-02): "go for the dashboard" after the plan in `docs/ux/dashboards-plan.md`. The plan's open decisions were not answered, so the recommended choice was taken for each and is recorded here; any can be changed later.

## Decisions taken
1. **No chart package.** The existing inline-SVG chart was promoted to `x-ui.chart` (adds `hbar`, `spark`, dashed second series, a text title and a table fallback). It works offline on a LAN, prints, and is accessible. `x-analytics.chart` now delegates to it, so the BI screens are unchanged.
2. **One tile.** The shared `x-ui.kpi-tile` (already used by Quality and Facility) was extended, not replaced: with only a label and a value it renders as before (tested). `x-analytics.kpi-tile` stays for the KPI catalog and moves over in D6.
3. **One number writer.** `App\Support\Dashboard\Numbers` writes every figure; `AnalyticsFormat` delegates to it. Defaults are unchanged (international grouping, English digits). A hospital chooses South Asian grouping (12,34,567 — how taka are usually written) and Bangla digits in Settings. **Recommendation for the pilot:** switch grouping to `south_asian`; Bangla digits are the hospital's call (D5 in the plan).
4. **Targets are the hospital's.** None ships (`dashboard.target.<code>`); a figure without a target has no good/bad colour. Status is always colour + icon + word.
5. **Refresh rhythm.** Live boards 60 s, management pages 300 s (`config/ux.php`), a board never reloads while someone is typing or the tab is hidden. Pages show "as of" and a Refresh button. (D3 in the plan: change in `config/ux.php`.)
6. **Cache** (60 s) keyed by hospital, branch, period and the viewer's rights, so one scope never sees another's figures; the time worked out is kept and shown.
7. **Periods** are Dhaka days (today, 7, 30, 90 days, this month, last month, custom up to 366 days); queries on UTC columns use `fromUtc()/toUtc()`; "against the period before" is the equal stretch before (the whole previous month for month periods).
8. **Reference build:** the Welfare dashboard, with two new list pages (gifts, discounts) that each figure opens and whose count and total a test compares with the figure.

## D1 — the front door (built)
1. **Home (`/dashboard`)** now opens with "What needs me now?": counts of the work the person's rights allow and the hospital's modules have on, most waiting first, the clear ones named underneath, each opening the list it counts (`HomeTiles`). Counts that depend on the person are theirs: a discount or a welfare decision the person asked for is never counted as theirs to approve; unread notices and quality actions are their own. Worked out per person and kept for a minute. The role's worklist (`role_homes`) is a button, and the old widgets and "current context" stay below for administrators.
2. **Today in the hospital (`/admin/today`, new right `dashboard.today.view`, new role `hospital_director`)**: outpatients, emergency arrivals, inpatients and bed occupancy, theatre cases, billed and collected, alerts waiting, discounts and grants waiting. Each money and visit figure is set against the **average of the same weekday over the last four weeks** and has a 14-day trend; days are Dhaka days (a day starts at 18:00 UTC the evening before). Counts and money only: a test proves no patient name appears. Reloads every 5 minutes.
3. **Queue board (`/admin/queue-board`, existing right `appointments.queue`)**: waiting now, longest wait, average wait before being called, seen, did not come, doctors with patients, and a table by doctor; **token numbers only, never names**, so it can be put on a wall; reloads every 60 seconds; the hospital may set `dashboard.target.opd_longest_wait`.
4. **Known limits:** the Today tiles open each module's board, not an exact list (the module boards are rebuilt on the standard in D2–D5, where the figure-equals-list test applies). Demo data ends yesterday, so "today" figures read as an early morning. Branch filter is not offered on Today (the director sees the hospital).

## D2 — the clinical boards (built)
**IPD (Indoor)**, **Nursing**, **ICU**, **OT / Surgery** and **Emergency performance** now follow the standard: one question and audience each, KPI tiles with change and (where the hospital sets one) a target, charts, an "as of" stamp, caching, and links only to pages the person may open. Each module's figures moved into a service (`IpdOverview`, `NursingOverview`, `IcuOverview`, `OtOverview`, `EdPerformance`) as plain arrays; the controllers keep their routes, rights and view data names, so nothing that read them changed.
- **IPD:** occupancy is of the *usable* beds (out of service and maintenance are not), the same definition for the whole hospital and for each ward; admissions and discharges against the same weekday over four weeks; expected and delayed discharges; waiting requests and transfers; admitted-and-discharged trend.
- **Nursing:** a task is **overdue when it is still to do and its time has gone**, whether or not its status says so (the old figure counted only the status `overdue`, which the system rarely sets, so it showed zero while many tasks were late); a medicine is overdue when it was due and not given. Counts are the nurse's own where they say "my"; the figures are kept per nurse, not shared across nurses. The page reloads every 60 seconds.
- **ICU:** occupancy of ICU beds only; alerts nobody has acknowledged and how long the oldest has waited; alerts per day. The unanswered-alert list names patients because it is the clinical team's own worklist (not part of the cached figures).
- **OT:** "cases today" are the ones that still stand (cancelled and postponed have their own figure); **average late start** and **cases called off (30 days)** are the two measures a theatre manager is judged on; a figure that cannot be worked out is a dash, never a false zero.
- **Emergency performance:** the department's own medians and 90th percentiles (unchanged service) set against the period before; arrivals by day and by hour of the day in Dhaka time; the old `?days=` links still work. Targets: `dashboard.target.ed_door_to_triage`, `ed_door_to_doctor`, `ed_seen_within_target`, `ed_lwbs`; IPD `ipd_occupancy`; OT `ot_start_delay`.
- A figure that cannot be worked out now shows a dash on every tile (this was blank before).
Tiles open the module's own list pages where one exists; an exact figure-equals-list proof for those lists belongs to the slices that rebuild the lists (a list that states its count, as Welfare's does).

## D3 — Laboratory, Radiology, Pharmacy and Blood bank
- **Laboratory:** work waiting at each stage (the stages add up to "tests to work on"), samples not yet in, critical results nobody has acknowledged and how long the oldest has waited, reports finished today against the same weekday of the last four weeks. **Turnaround = from the order to the pathologist's last approval on that order**, shown as a median and a 90th percentile (nearest rank, whole minutes) against the period before. Cancelled orders are not counted. Sample rejection is a share of samples taken in the period.
- **Radiology:** patients waiting for a scan, scans in progress, **scanned but not reported** with the age of the oldest and how many are urgent or stat, critical findings unseen, reports waiting for approval. **Report time = from the scan being finished to the approved final report**; studies by type of scan and per day.
- **Pharmacy:** prescriptions waiting and under review, safety alerts nobody has acted on, batches near expiry that still have stock, batches set aside, and **medicines with nothing usable on the shelf** (a quarantined or expired batch is not usable). **Wait = order to first dispensing.**
- **Blood bank:** usable bags by product against the policy minimum, bags expiring inside the warning window, requests waiting (and how many are emergency), bags issued today, bags on the ward not yet started, open incidents. **Wait = request to first bag issued**; wastage = bags thrown away or expired as a share of bags received (the existing utilization report, not a second definition).
- A time that cannot be worked out (nothing finished in the period) is a dash, never zero. No page names a patient in its cached figures; the blood bank's request and incident lists, which name patients and bags, are the team's own worklists and are read live.
- Targets (none ship): `dashboard.target.lab_tat`, `radiology_tat`, `pharmacy_wait`, `blood_wait`.
- The dashboards default to the last 7 days; the lab, radiology and pharmacy dashboards showed only "today" before.
