# D-P17 — Patient Portal, Mobile and CRM: state and decisions

Status at the end of the build: see `docs/phase17/gate-f2-report.md`. The 118-section Phase 17 spec is not in the repository; the guideline's section 26.6 and the slice table were the brief, and every number below is an example for the hospital to confirm (OD-17).

## Decisions
1. **One account table.** `patient_portal_accounts` (Phase 1) is extended, not replaced. One account per patient; a relative's access is a `portal_proxies` row linking *another* account to the patient. The old `user_id` column stays, unused.
2. **No enumeration.** Registration and password reset return identical bodies for a known and an unknown person; limits are counted on the phone number and the address whether or not anyone matched.
3. **One-time codes.** Six digits, kept as a keyed hash, ten-minute life, five tries, resend limits. The code goes to the phone number *on the patient record*. A registration with a different number is a front-desk job: the desk checks identity and hands over a one-time invitation code (valid 14 days), claimed with the date of birth.
4. **Two ways in, one rule.** The light web pages use the `portal` session guard; the mobile app uses Sanctum tokens whose owner is the account and whose only ability is `portal`. Staff routes refuse patient tokens (`staff.only`), patient routes refuse staff; the hospital comes from the account, never from a header or the browser session. While a patient request runs, `auth()->id()` is "nobody" so staff services never record a patient id as a user id.
5. **A service user stands in** where staff services want "the user who did it" (booking, cashier): one locked, login-less `Patient portal (service)` user per organization. The real actor is the account id in the audit trail and the record's `source`.
6. **Release.** `portal_release_policies` (default, per lab category, radiology) and one `portal_result_releases` row per report. Strictest rule wins; sensitive words force "after a clinician"; a critical result is never released by a timer or policy: its alert must be acknowledged and a clinician releases it. Only recent reports (30 days) are picked up automatically; an old backlog is brought in on purpose with `portal:check --since-days=N`.
7. **Money.** `portal_payments` has a unique idempotency key per account; one payment in progress per bill; only a verified provider outcome calls `PaymentService::collect`; a bill settled meanwhile marks the online payment "to refund"; the settlement file is matched line by line (matched / amount differs / unknown / not succeeded).
8. **Messages are not orders**, urgent text is flagged and the sender told to call the emergency number, nothing in a message creates an order.
9. **Consent per channel and purpose**: `transactional` cannot be switched off; `service` is on until switched off; `marketing` is off until switched on; quiet hours (21:00–08:00 Asia/Dhaka) hold texts and push; at most two marketing messages per week. A diagnosis-based group needs a written legal basis, a second approver and the patient's clinical-marketing consent.
10. **Documents.** A shared link is a random 48-character token kept as a hash, expires (7 days), can be limited to a number of opens, can be revoked, needs the signed-in owner (or an approved relative with the documents scope) and is logged on every open.
11. **Relatives.** Requested by the relative, approved by staff with scopes and an end date; a parent's approval ends at the child's 18th birthday; for a 13–17 year old, results and messages stay hidden from the parent whatever the approval says (config).
12. **Telehealth and AI** are interfaces plus null/sandbox adapters only (Phase 24 and Phase 20).

## Deferred / needs people (not code)
- **Adapters:** a real SMS provider (until then sign-in codes wait in the outbox and **registration cannot reach patients' phones** — connect one before go-live), a real payment aggregator (bKash / Nagad / card) with a webhook signature check, push delivery (FCM / APNs; device tokens are stored encrypted), a video provider. Email sending is not wired (rows wait in the outbox).
- **Confirmations:** every number in `config/portal.php`; the release policy per test category (especially HIV, pregnancy, genetic, histopathology); the age for an own account (16) and the adolescent privacy rule; complaint deadlines; quiet hours; retention periods; terms of use, privacy notice and consent wording (a lawyer / the privacy officer).
- **People work:** native Bangla review of every patient-facing string; accessibility audit with real assistive technology and real patients on low-cost phones; a penetration test of the portal and the mobile API; a load test of the booking path; a restore drill covering `portal_*` tables.
- **Not built:** a PACS viewer (a link is built from the `portal.viewer_url` setting); the mobile app itself and its offline queue (the API gives `sync`, ETags, idempotency keys and paging); patient-to-patient or doctor-to-patient *clinical* chat (by design); AI replies; online insurance claim tracking for the patient (the policy is shown, claim status is not); campaign A/B tests and unsubscribe links in email; fine-grained per-test release overrides for a single patient.
- **Next to watch:** the first week after opening — messages past their reply time, held critical results, "to refund" payments, locked logins and the reconciliation differences are all on the portal dashboard.
