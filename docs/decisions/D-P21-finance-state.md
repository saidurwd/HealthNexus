# D-P21 — Finance and Accounting: state and decisions

Status at the end of the build: see `docs/phase21/gate-f2-report.md`. The guideline's section 27.1 and the slice table (S00–S12) were the brief. **OD-05: build.** Every number is an example for the hospital's accountant to confirm; **no tax rate is built in** (OD-03).

## Decisions
1. **Billing stays the source of truth for patient charges; Finance is the source of truth for accounting.** Finance reads other modules' records and posts; it never changes them. Other modules never write to the ledger except through `FinanceGateway` and the listeners.
2. **A journal is balanced or it does not exist.** Debits equal credits before it is saved for approval; every line is one-sided; control accounts (receivable, payable) need a named party. Posted journals are immutable — the **database** refuses an update or delete of their lines or of the journal (triggers on SQLite and MySQL) — and a mistake is undone by a reversal (a new journal, swapped sides, pointing back; the original is marked reversed, never edited).
3. **One posting per source and event.** `fin_journals` has a unique key (organization, source type, source id, event); the engine records each attempt in `fin_postings`. A failure is stored with its reason and retried; it never reaches the cashier.
4. **Rules are data.** Which account takes the revenue of a Billing category, the cash/wallet/card/bank account of a payment method, VAT, payroll lines — all in `fin_account_roles` (editable). A missing mapping is reported, never guessed.
5. **Open items are the sub-ledger.** Each invoice (or insurer claim) is one receivable item reduced by payments, refunds, credits, advances and approved write-offs; ageing and statements come from here, and a check compares them with the control accounts. Insurance: the insurer's share moves from the patient's account to the insurer's when the claim is accepted; an insurer's payment (Billing method `INS`) settles the claim; a disallowance goes to an expense account.
6. **Period control.** Months are open → locked → (with the year) closed. Automatic postings into a locked period wait in the failed list unless the hospital allows them; reopening a locked month needs a reason. Closing a year sweeps revenue and expense into retained earnings with one closing journal (excluded from the year's own income statement) and cannot be undone.
7. **Maker-checker** on manual journals, supplier payments, write-offs and budgets: the person who made it cannot approve it (setting; on by default).
8. **Dates** are the hospital's local dates (Asia/Dhaka); times stay UTC.
9. **Stock** is valued from the append-only stock ledger once the day is over (one journal per day and kind of movement) and goods receipts post against "goods received, not invoiced", which the supplier invoice then clears. Movements without a cost are counted and reported, not guessed.
10. **Cash and bank.** Closing a till can be banked once; a counted difference goes to cash over/short first so the books equal the real cash. Statements are imported from CSV (duplicates skipped), matched by amount and date to deposits, supplier payments and gateway settlements; the rest are booked by a person; a statement is reconciled only when nothing is unmatched and its closing balance equals the books.
11. **Fee sharing is rules, not spreadsheets**: a percent of net revenue per doctor and, if wanted, per Billing category (most specific rule wins); the month's shares are accrued once as an expense and a payable to the doctor.
12. **Budget check on purchases is a service** (`BudgetService::checkPurchase`) with a screen; hooking it into Procurement's order approval is a one-line change in Phase 15 code that this phase did not make (rule 15: another module's code).

## Found while building (for Billing and Portal, not changed here)
- **An insured patient can pay the insurer's share.** Billing still shows the whole invoice as due until the insurer pays, and the portal lets the patient pay that whole amount. If the insurer's claim is then written off, the books correctly show the patient's account **in credit** (an overpayment to refund). On the six-month MySQL build this happened on 26 of about 3,900 invoices. Decide in Billing/Portal whether the due shown to an insured patient should be only their own share.
- **Dates from other modules can lie in the future** (insurance write-off decisions were dated up to three weeks ahead in the demo). The books never take a date later than today.
