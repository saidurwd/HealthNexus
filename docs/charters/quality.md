# Quality, Safety & Compliance — Module Charter

_Updated in every slice (guideline 7.4). Phase 23 slices S00–S09 are built. Harm levels, due days, the outbreak threshold, indicator targets and the starter checklist are examples awaiting the hospital's quality committee._

- Phase / owner: 23 / **owner to be named by the hospital** (the quality manager with the medical director)
- Status: built · switchable per organization / hospital (`quality`, depends on core); every staff member can report an incident and read documents, the rest needs a quality right
- Purpose: one place for patient-safety and compliance work — incident and near-miss reports, investigations and corrective/preventive actions, infection control, quality indicators and clinical audit, review of every death, controlled documents, clinical privileges, the licence calendar, accreditation checklists, and the committees and morbidity-and-mortality meeting that act on all of it.
- Permission prefix: `quality` (35 permissions). Table prefix: `qa_`. Staff screens `/admin/quality/*`; read-only API `/api/v1/quality/*`.
- Owns: incidents with their amendment trail, event trail and (protected) reporter row; investigations; actions; complaint links; infection cases, isolations, hand-hygiene rounds and outbreaks; indicators and their monthly values; clinical audits; death reviews; documents, versions, assignments and acknowledgements; privilege list and privileges; the compliance calendar; checklists, assessments and results; committees, meetings and agenda items.
- Does NOT own: any clinical, billing, stock or HR record. It reads blood-bank incidents, medicine safety alerts, surgical checklists, deaths (ED and ward), patient complaints (CRM), staff licences (HR) and the Phase 19 warehouse, and never changes them.
- Rules: incidents are append-only with amendments (the reported facts are frozen by the model **and by database triggers**); the reporter is not on the incident — a named reporter has a row only `quality.incident.reveal` may open (a reason is required and every opening is recorded), an anonymous report leaves no row, no audit entry and no user on its first event; nothing assigns blame (causes are systems categories, there is no "person at fault"); investigations of serious incidents, actions, documents and privileges are each decided or checked by a second person; an approved document version is frozen (model and trigger) — a change is a new version; a death gets exactly one review.
- Events/hooks: no events published; the sweep (`quality:check`, daily 06:00) reads other modules' tables. `IsolationGateway`-style questions are answered by `InfectionService::activePrecautions()` and `PrivilegeService::holds()` — other modules ask these, they do not read the tables.
- Status machines: incident reported → triaged → investigating → actioned → closed (reopen → triaged/investigating); investigation open → submitted → accepted | returned; action open → in_progress → done → verified (or back to open); document version draft → in_review → approved → superseded | rejected; privilege requested → granted → expired | suspended | revoked | declined; infection case suspected → confirmed | ruled_out; outbreak open → contained → closed; death review pending → reviewed → discussed; meeting planned → held → minuted; assessment in_progress → completed.
- Settings keys: `quality.*` override `config/quality.php` (categories, harm levels, due days, investigation threshold, anonymity, infection thresholds, review days, document and privilege rules, warning days).
- Reports / KPIs: 13 standard quality indicators (readmission, repeat operation, inpatient mortality, deaths reviewed in time, falls, pressure injuries, medication events, hospital-acquired infections, hand hygiene, reporting rate, near-miss share, incidents and actions closed on time) with hospital-set targets; overview, lists and a read-only API.
- Known limitations & deferred items: see docs/decisions/D-P23-quality-state.md.
