# AI & Clinical Intelligence — Module Charter

_Updated in every slice (guideline 7.4). Phase 20 is built for the framework and five low-risk functions; Phase 20b adds four functions above low risk (medication review facts, radiology reading order, claim pre-check, early-warning screen), all off until switched on by two people. Risk classes, thresholds, injection patterns, forbidden phrases and the review window are examples awaiting the hospital's AI governance committee._

- Phase / owner: 20 / **owner to be named by the hospital** (the AI governance lead, with a clinical reviewer and a safety officer)
- Status: built · switchable per organization / hospital (`ai`, depends on analytics); **off until a person switches it on**. Everything is a draft; nothing is written to a record by AI.
- Purpose: let clinicians and staff use AI to read, summarise, draft, answer and forecast — safely, with every request logged, every output labelled "AI-generated — needs review", and a person always deciding.
- Permission prefix: `ai` (21 permissions). Table prefix: `ai_`. Staff screens `/admin/ai/*` (45 routes); read-only API `/api/v1/ai/{use-cases|outputs|metrics}`.
- Owns: providers and models (the register of every outside service that could receive data), use cases (owner, risk class, data class, model), prompt versions, approved knowledge items, evaluation sets/cases/runs, incidents, the request log, outputs (drafts), feedback.
- Does NOT own: clinical records (AI only reads them through Patients/Clinical services; it never writes there), the analytics warehouse and its semantic layer (Analytics — natural-language questions call Analytics' own `QuestionService`), policies and procedures (Quality documents — Knowledge reads only approved versions), stock (Inventory — the forecast reads the stock ledger and orders nothing).
- Rules (guideline 10.10): AI may inform, summarise, draft, suggest and forecast. It may never diagnose, prescribe, change doses, order, assign final triage, discharge, transfer, override alerts, finalise notes or reports, change demographics, bill or claim. `AiGateway` is the only door and checks in this order: master switch → use case enabled and built → `ai.use` → provider enabled and model approved → data class and mode (identifiable or de-identified) → instruction-like text → provider call → `OutputGuard` → draft. Every call writes an immutable `ai_requests` row (completed, blocked or failed). A provider abroad receives de-identified data only unless a written legal approval is recorded.
- Guards: `OutputGuard` drops lines with no source, with another request's source, with numbers not in the cited facts, or that tell someone to act (English and Bangla); URLs are removed. `InjectionGuard` scores untrusted text and quotes it as data. `Deidentifier` removes names, phones and emails. `ContextEngine` is read-only and checks `ai.use`, `patients.view`, the same hospital, and the right patient.
- Approval: a model is approved only after an evaluation run with recall ≥ 0.9, no unsupported line, no forbidden leak, at least 5 cases, English and Bangla both covered. A function above low risk needs a second approver; `pause` is the kill switch; the master switch stops everything.
- Review: `OutputService::accept` needs `ai.output.review` and the patient number typed again (a mismatch opens a `wrong_patient` incident); an edited version is kept next to the original; nothing is written to a record. Drafts lapse after 72 hours.
- Hooks: `ai:check` daily at 06:45 (lapses old drafts, acceptance and drift monitoring); `ai:install`; `ai:redteam` (the red-team pack, a gate before any model is trusted).
- Functions built, low risk (switched on by `ai:install`/Set up the local engine): patient timeline summary, clinical note draft (facts only; assessment and plan left for the clinician), hospital knowledge answers, inventory demand forecast, natural-language analytics.
- Functions built, above low risk (Phase 20b; **off until the owner sets them up and a second person switches them on, and only after the model has passed that function's own evaluation**): medication review support (high; allergies, active medicines, Pharmacy's own safety alerts and counts side by side — never says to start, stop or change anything), radiology worklist support (medium; suggests a reading order by priority then waiting time, the Radiology worklist is not touched), insurance claim pre-check (medium; restates the Insurance module's own scrub rules, documents, payer queries, deadline — never submits or decides), clinical risk scores (high; an open NEWS2 screen from stored vital signs, every input and point shown, consciousness and extra oxygen shown as not scored, so the total is "at least"; starts no alert or escalation).
- Registered but not built, cannot be switched on: laboratory interpretation, ICU early warning, emergency triage support, patient portal assistant.
- Roles ready to use: `ai_governance_lead`, `ai_clinical_reviewer`, `ai_safety_officer`; `ai.use` is also given to nursing, pharmacy, store, finance, radiology and claims roles. A function reads other modules only through their own right too: the claim check needs `insurance.claim.view`, the reading order needs `radiology.worklist.view`, the medicine sheet and the screen need `patients.view` in the patient's hospital.
- Settings keys: `ai.*` override `config/ai.php`.
- Reports / KPIs: drafts, acceptance rate, errors reported, speed, drift (a drop of 20 points or more with at least 10 reviewed in both windows), blocked requests, incidents.
- Known limitations & deferred items: see docs/decisions/D-P20-ai-state.md.

## Tables
`ai_providers`, `ai_models`, `ai_use_cases`, `ai_prompts`, `ai_knowledge`, `ai_eval_sets`, `ai_eval_cases`, `ai_eval_runs`, `ai_incidents`, `ai_requests` (immutable by trigger), `ai_outputs` (generated fields frozen by trigger, no delete), `ai_feedback`.
