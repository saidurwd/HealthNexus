# Inventory / Procurement — Module Charter

_Updated in every slice (guideline 7.4). Phase 15 slices S00–S13 are built; every threshold, approval level and tolerance is an example awaiting the stores and procurement managers (OD-15)._

- Phase / owner: 15 / **owner to be named by the hospital** (stores manager and procurement manager)
- Status: built · switchable per organization / hospital (`inventory`, depends on core)
- Purpose: keep the hospital's non-drug supplies (surgical consumables, dressings, linen, stationery, housekeeping, equipment spares) in stock — buy them through approved requisitions, quotations and purchase orders, receive and inspect them, move and issue them, count and write them off with a second person, and match supplier invoices before anything goes to payment.
- Permission prefixes: `inventory` and `procurement` (see docs/registry/permissions.md). Table prefixes: `inventory_`, `procurement_`. Routes: `/admin/inventory/*` and `/admin/procurement/*`. API: none yet.
- Owns: item master with units of measure and conversions, categories, stores and shelves, batches and expiry, the stock projection (`inventory_stock`), serial records, suppliers with documents / scorecards / agreed prices, requisitions, requests for quotation, purchase orders (versions, emergency orders), goods receipts and inspection, supplier invoices and the three-way match, supplier returns, department budgets, transfers, department requests and issues, point-of-care use, counts, adjustments, holds and recalls, the read-only views `v_stock_valuation` and `v_expiry_risk`.
- Does NOT own: **drugs** (Phase 7 keeps its drug master, pharmacy stock and dispensing; an item may point at a medication through `medication_id`, nothing is copied); **blood units** (Phase 13); **prices and charges** (Billing — patient-linked use publishes a ChargeEvent keyed `INV:<visit>:<code>:<id>`; Inventory never knows a price); **the general ledger and payables** (the payable is handed to `FinanceGateway::postPayable`, null adapter until Phase 21); the patient (MPI); users and departments (core).
- Stock rule: nothing changes stock except `InventoryStockService`, which writes the append-only `stock_ledger` (store kind `inventory`, a database trigger refuses UPDATE and DELETE) and the projection in the same transaction. The projection can be rebuilt from the ledger (`InventoryLedgerReconciler`). Stock never goes negative unless the store says so; a held, recalled, expired or out-of-date batch cannot be issued; oldest expiry goes first.
- Procurement chain: requisition → approval by amount (levels 1–3, each a different person, never the requester) → request for quotation and comparison (or direct order) → purchase order (approval by amount, budget check, supplier must be qualified, versioned amendments) → goods receipt (counted, then inspected: accepted units stocked, rejected units to the quarantine store, short shelf life refused) → supplier invoice (three-way match against order and accepted goods, tolerance from settings) → hand-over to Finance only when matched or approved by a second person → goods returned and credited.
- Emergency orders: allowed up to a ceiling, issued at once, must be approved afterwards by the same chain within a time limit; an overdue one shows on the dashboard; refusing it stops what has not arrived. They still go through receiving, inspection and matching.
- Four-eyes: adjustments, count variances, mismatched invoices and approvals each need a person other than the requester. Counts post only when a different person approves.
- Hooks: nothing is listened to; the daily `inventory:check` command (06:30) blocks expired batches, proposes their write-off, warns about stock and supplier documents about to expire and about emergency orders not approved in time.
- Settings keys: `inventory.*` override `config/inventory.php` (approval levels, emergency ceiling and hours, match tolerance, over-receipt, shelf-life rule, expiry alert windows, reorder look-back, count note threshold, reason codes, document types).
- Reports: dashboard, stock on hand, value of stock by store and category, expiry risk, use by department (issues plus point-of-care use), slow movers, supplier spend / score / rejection rate, reorder suggestions from real use (CSV export with formula-safe cells).
- Known limitations & deferred items: see docs/decisions/D-P15-inventory-state.md.
