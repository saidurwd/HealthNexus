# Support and escalation sheet (fill in before go-live)

| Level | Who | How to reach | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — Floor-walker / super-user | named per department | phone / WhatsApp group | "How do I…?", small problems, anything on a screen |
| 2 — Hospital IT lead | | phone (24 h during hypercare) | system slow or down, printer/scanner, user locked out, a wrong record needs correcting |
| 3 — Vendor support | | phone + e-mail | defects, data questions, anything touching patient safety or money |
| 4 — Management / clinical lead | | phone | patient-safety event, data breach suspected, go/no-go for downtime |

**What to say:** the screen name, what you were doing, the **reference code** shown on an error screen, and the time. Do not send patient names by chat; give the patient number.

| Severity | Example | First answer within | Aim to fix within |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Critical | system down; wrong patient/medicine risk; money not balancing | 15 minutes, 24 h | 4 hours or workaround |
| 2 High | one module unusable; critical alerts not arriving | 1 hour | 1 working day |
| 3 Normal | wrong wording, slow screen, report mismatch | 1 working day | 5 working days |
| 4 Request | new report, new user, training | 2 working days | agreed |

(Numbers are examples: set them with the hospital.) Hypercare: daily 15-minute stand-up for two weeks after go-live; defects triaged by severity; patient-safety and money defects interrupt everything.
